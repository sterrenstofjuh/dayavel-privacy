Dayavel privacy policy

Short

Dayavel stores your data on your own phone. There is no Dayavel account and no Dayavel server. Dayavel shows no ads and does not sell data. Internet is only used for Google Play (subscription), Open Food Facts (product info, if you turn it on) and diagnostics by Google libraries.

What data

Everything stays locally on this device, in the app's private storage (including SharedPreferences "ritme" and "ringhub", and local files such as readings.json). Dayavel keeps what you enter or link, for example:

  • profile and preferences
  • agenda and shifts
  • busy times from your phone calendar (only if you turn this link on; start, end and all-day only, no title or details)
  • food, water, recipes and any recipe photos
  • sleep, training, weight and goals
  • progress photos (only in the app's internal storage; they are not included in backup or export)
  • steps from your phone's step counter (only if you turn this on yourself, for example without Health Connect)
  • Health Connect data (only with your permission; see below)
  • data from an optional Bluetooth ring (directly via Bluetooth: heart rate, steps, sleep and battery; stored locally) or smartwatch (via Health Connect)
  • local crash log lines (until you share them)

There is no Dayavel account.

Health Connect

Dayavel asks, only if you grant permission, for read access to these types (as in the Android manifest and the Health Connect code): steps, heart rate, sleep, distance, oxygen saturation, respiratory rate and heart rate variability. Steps, heart rate and sleep are selected by default in the chooser; you tick the other types yourself. Dayavel does not write to Health Connect. That data stays on your device. You can revoke access in Android settings under Health Connect.

Dayavel's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect policy of Google, including the Limited Use requirements. Dayavel does not use this data for advertising, does not sell it and does not share it with third parties.

Phone calendar (optional)

Only if you turn the link on (off by default) and grant permission may Dayavel read your phone calendar. Dayavel reads only the start, end and whether it is an all-day event, to show busy blocks in your agenda. No title, description, location or other details. Dayavel does not write anything back to your calendar. Turning the link off stops the reading. The on/off choice stays on the device and is not included in the backup.

Location

Location is used only when you start a walk with location, and on Android 11 and older when searching for a Bluetooth ring. The location permission is declared in the Android manifest. Only when you start a walk with location do location points stay in memory; they are discarded at the end of the session: no storage, no export, no backup of the route. Only duration and distance may remain in your training log. Dayavel does not send location to a Dayavel server. There is no background location. On Android 11 and older, Android may also require location permission for Bluetooth scan; Dayavel does not store that location as app data.

Open Food Facts (optional)

Online product info is off by default. If you enable it and search or scan yourself, Dayavel sends the barcode or search term over HTTPS to Open Food Facts (world.openfoodfacts.org and search.openfoodfacts.org). No account, weight or food log is sent. The User-Agent names only the app name and version, with no personal data. Open Food Facts sees your IP address, as with any internet connection. Retrieved product info may be kept locally.

Google Play (subscription)

Payments go through Google Play Billing. Dayavel does not store card details or the purchase token, only the subscription status, timestamps, the chosen plan and a hash of the token, locally, so the app can work offline for a while. You manage cancellation and payment details in Google Play.

Camera, Bluetooth, OCR and photos

  • Camera and gallery: for the live barcode scanner, food labels, recipe photos, shopping photos, roster photos and progress photos. Images are processed on device (ML Kit barcode and text recognition). Photos and OCR results are not sent to a Dayavel server.
  • Roster photos are temporary: after recognition the app deletes the temporary file; only the shifts you save are kept.
  • Recipe and shopping photos are stored in the app's private storage; they may be included in the backup ZIP.
  • Progress photos stay only in the app's internal storage and are not included in backup or export.
  • Google libraries in the app (ML Kit for barcode and text recognition, Google Play Billing) may send technical diagnostic data to Google, such as device type, app version and an installation ID. This does not include photos, scan results or health data.
  • Bluetooth: to find and connect an optional ring. Dayavel reads heart rate, steps, sleep and battery from the ring and stores them locally. Dayavel does not store location via Bluetooth.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled locally (AlarmManager). There is no Dayavel push server.

Crash logs

Crash and error log lines stay on your device. They are shared only if you use the share sheet from About Dayavel or the crash card.

Retention

Dayavel keeps your data on this device until you erase it via Erase data, or until you uninstall the app. There is no automatic deletion after a fixed period. Automatic cloud backup and device transfer are off in the app. The app does occasionally make an automatic ZIP backup in the app folder on this phone (not in the cloud); that helps if something goes wrong in the app, not if you lose or uninstall the app. You keep any manual export or backup yourself; Dayavel does not erase those files for you afterwards. Progress photos are not included in export or backup.

Your rights

You can view your data in the app itself. The app also has these functions (under More > Privacy and data):

  • Export data: you create a zip with JSON and CSV yourself and choose where to save it.
  • Erase data: you can erase everything, or only the ring measurements, each time with confirmation.

You withdraw Health Connect access in Android settings, not through a separate function in Dayavel. You turn off the phone calendar link in the app.

Export and backup

You create export (JSON/CSV in a zip) and backup yourself; you choose where to store the file. The export file and the backup are not encrypted. Keep them in a safe place. Progress photos and walk location points are not included in export or backup. Recipe and shopping photos may be included in the backup ZIP.

Permissions in the Android manifest (honest)

Dayavel declares among others: INTERNET (Play Billing and optional Open Food Facts), Health Connect read permissions (the seven types above), Bluetooth (ring), CAMERA (scanner/photos), notifications, boot completed (reschedule reminders), ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION (steps/cadence and the phone's step counter), ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION and ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION (only when you start a walk with location, and Bluetooth scan on Android 11 and older), FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION (only during an active session; no background location), READ_CALENDAR (optional phone calendar, read only), wake lock and alarm permission. No ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION, no WRITE_CALENDAR. Not every permission is always active; many only apply after your permission or action.

What we do not do

  • No required Dayavel account
  • No ads in the app
  • No selling your data to advertisers
  • No Dayavel-owned analytics or tracking service
